# Verification record

Publication date: 2026-10-20. Product: Spark Stand Dual only.

The four projects are byte-preserved copies of the owner-identified FINAL_PRINTED bundle. Only the download filenames changed from X2D_ASA to X2D_PLA. This corrects the name to the saved filament profile; it does not establish physical material suitability.

Prior geometry review matched all 14 parts against retained Dual STL geometry: two v04 panels, eight v04 wedges, three plain v05 lighter bars and one logo bar. Prior release-preparation work recorded successful fresh CLI slicing of all four projects using Bambu Studio 2.8.2.61. The original physical print jobs are not independently identified by saved G-code.

Publication checks reconfirmed archive integrity, saved X2D/PLA settings and absence of ready-to-run .gcode/.bgcode members. SHA256SUMS records the exact distributed files.

The prior CLI run reported T65279/T65535 diagnostics. The investigation traced those commands to the stock X2D end profile and a limitation of the G-code analyzer. Commands have not been deleted or edited. This is not a firmware or printer-operation certification.

GUI project opening and Preview have not been verified for all four files. Recipients must select profiles matching their own equipment, slice anew and inspect Preview and warnings before printing. No printer job was sent during release preparation.

Physical assembly and placement of one device on the lower tier are evidenced in the private development record. Full-frame load capacity, thermal response, creep and long-term reliability remain unverified. The 591.1 g measurement is stand mass, not a load rating.

The distribution excludes private development history, CAD sources, Modular files, unrelated personal photographs and internal audit records. Original project metadata is preserved; LICENSE.md supplies the distribution terms because the native archives contain no embedded reuse license.

On 2026-10-20 the owner authorized including two product photographs in the public repository. They document assembly and one-device placement only.
