# Dual render images

Project-authored renders generated on 2026-10-17. The stand is Dual v04 with solid crossbars; the distributed print bundle instead includes v05 LIGHTER open crossbars, one with a logo. The images illustrate the overall arrangement rather than the exact released bar geometry.

Computer props are procedural visualization meshes created in this project, not official NVIDIA CAD. No imported device meshes, image textures, fonts or HDRIs are used. NVIDIA product identity and trademarks remain with their owners; no endorsement is implied. These images do not establish physical load or thermal performance.

Original render filenames are preserved. Restricted project terms in ../../LICENSE.md apply to the distributed images; no rights to underlying third-party trademarks or product designs are granted.
